Cover Maker — an offline browser app that turns your photos into GTA-style multi-panel cover art: the iconic poster / loading-screen look with a Vice City neon-sunset grade. Pick 1–4 panels, crop and pan each photo, tune the sunset grade, halftone and vignette, letter the caption, and export a PNG in one step — 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. No network requests, photos never leave your machine. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "GTA 6 Cover Art" bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — upload up to 4 photos (or use the included samples), choose a layout, drag on the cover to pan a panel, and hit Export PNG.
